Frequently Asked Questions

How does background removal work?

An AI model analyzes the image to estimate which pixels belong to the main subject and which belong to the background. It then creates an image with transparent background pixels.

Are my photos uploaded to a server?

This tool is designed to process images locally in your browser. It downloads the AI library and model files when needed, but it does not intentionally upload your selected photo to a DigiiFox processing server. The library and model are loaded from a third-party CDN, so their hosting provider may receive normal network metadata.

Why does the first run take longer?

The browser may need to download the AI library and model files before it can process your image. Later runs may be faster if those resources remain cached.

Why are some edges imperfect?

Fine hair, transparent objects, motion blur, low contrast, and busy backgrounds can be difficult for AI models. Results vary depending on the image and model.

Which format should I download?

PNG is recommended when you need transparency. WebP can also preserve transparency with a smaller file size in many cases. JPG cannot store transparency and requires a solid background.

Can I use the cutout for product photos?

Yes. You can use a transparent cutout in product listings, presentations, posters, thumbnails, and other designs. Check the result before publishing, especially for important commercial images.
