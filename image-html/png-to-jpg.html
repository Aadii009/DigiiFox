Frequently Asked Questions

Learn more about converting PNG images to JPG.

Is this PNG to JPG converter free?

Yes. You can use this browser-based converter without creating an account.

Will converting PNG to JPG reduce quality?

JPG uses lossy compression, so some image information may be lost. A higher quality setting generally reduces visible compression, though it may increase file size.

What happens to transparent areas?

JPG does not support transparency. Transparent areas in your PNG are filled with white during conversion.

Are my images uploaded to a server?

This tool performs conversion locally in your browser. The conversion code does not intentionally upload your image to a server.

Can I convert a large PNG image?

The tool accepts files up to 25 MB and limits image dimensions to approximately 40 megapixels to help avoid excessive browser memory use.

Can I use the converted JPG commercially?

The converter does not change your ownership rights. Make sure you have permission to use the original image for your intended purpose.
