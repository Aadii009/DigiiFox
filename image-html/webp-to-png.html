Frequently Asked Questions

Is this WebP to PNG converter free?

Yes. You can convert WebP images to PNG for free using this browser-based tool.

Will my image transparency be preserved?

Yes. PNG supports transparency, and the converter preserves transparent areas when the browser can decode them correctly.

Will converting WebP to PNG improve image quality?

No. Converting formats does not restore details lost in an earlier lossy conversion. PNG can preserve the decoded image without adding further lossy compression.

Are my images uploaded to a server?

No. Conversion happens in your browser. Your image stays on your device during the conversion process.

Why is my PNG file larger than the WebP file?

PNG often creates larger files because it uses a different compression method. This can be normal, especially for photographs.

What is the maximum supported file size?

This tool accepts WebP files up to 25 MB and limits image dimensions to help avoid excessive browser memory use.
