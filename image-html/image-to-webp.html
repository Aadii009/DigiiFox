Frequently Asked Questions

What is the WebP image format?

WebP is an image format developed by Google that supports lossy and lossless compression, as well as transparency. It can help websites deliver smaller images, although the size savings depend on the source image and settings.

Is this Image to WebP converter free?

Yes. You can convert images to WebP using DigiiFox without paying for the tool.

Will WebP always make my image smaller?

No. WebP often reduces file size, but the result depends on the original format, image content, and quality setting. Some converted images may be larger than their originals.

Can I convert transparent PNG images?

Yes. WebP supports transparency, so transparent areas in a PNG can be preserved when the browser encodes the image correctly.

Does converting an image reduce quality?

WebP quality settings below 100 use lossy compression, which can change image details. Choose a higher quality setting when visual fidelity is important. Conversion from an already compressed image can introduce additional quality loss.

Are my images uploaded to a server?

No. The image is processed in your browser and is not sent to a DigiiFox server by this tool. The page does require your browser to support WebP encoding.

What is the maximum supported file size?

The tool accepts images up to 25 MB and 40 megapixels. Large images can require considerable memory, depending on your device and browser.
