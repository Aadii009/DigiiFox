Frequently Asked Questions

What are image filters?

Image filters adjust visual properties such as brightness, contrast, color intensity and hue to change the appearance of a photo.

Can I use multiple filters together?

Yes. You can combine brightness, contrast, saturation, grayscale, sepia, blur, hue rotation and color inversion.

Can I restore my original image?

Yes. Select the Original preset or click Reset All to remove your adjustments.

Which image formats are supported?

You can upload JPG, PNG and WebP images and export your edited result in any of those three formats.

Will filters reduce image quality?

Filters are rendered at the image's original dimensions. PNG is lossless, while JPG and WebP quality settings affect compression.

Are my images uploaded to a server?

No. This tool applies filters directly in your browser and does not upload your image to a DigiiFox server.

Does this tool use AI?

No. These are standard image-processing filters, not AI-based image enhancement.
