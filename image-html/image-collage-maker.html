How to Use Image Collage Maker

  1. Click Select Images and choose at least two photos.
  2. Arrange the photos using the up and down arrows. Remove any photo you don't need.
  3. Choose the number of columns and your preferred canvas shape.
  4. Adjust spacing, corner radius, and background color.
  5. Click Create Collage to generate the preview.
  6. Choose PNG, JPG, or WebP and click Download Collage.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is this Image Collage Maker free?

Yes. You can create and download collages without paying for this tool.

Are my photos uploaded to a server?

This tool processes selected photos locally in your browser. It does not intentionally upload your photos to a DigiiFox server. The page itself must still be loaded from wherever the website is hosted.

How many photos can I add?

You can add up to 15 photos, with a maximum file size of 15 MB per image. Very large images may use significant memory while processing.

Which formats can I download?

You can export the collage as PNG, JPG, or WebP. JPG does not support transparency, so the selected background color is used.

Can I change the order of my photos?

Yes. Use the up and down arrows in the Your Photos section to rearrange the images, then create the collage again.

Does this tool use AI?

No. It arranges your photos into a grid. It does not generate new images or use AI to edit the content of your photos.
