Why convert to PNG?

PNG is a lossless image format that is useful for graphics, screenshots, and images that need repeated editing.

Lossless format

PNG encoding does not add further lossy compression.

Transparency support

PNG supports transparency, but a JPG's solid background stays solid.

Easy to download

Save the converted PNG directly to your device.

i
Good to know

PNG files can be larger than JPG files. Converting a JPG to PNG does not restore image details lost during the original JPG compression.

Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions about JPG to PNG conversion.

Is the JPG to PNG converter free?

Yes. This browser-based tool is designed for free image conversion without installing a separate application.

Will converting JPG to PNG improve image quality?

No. The conversion does not recover details lost when the JPG was originally compressed. PNG avoids adding further lossy compression during the conversion.

Will my PNG file be smaller than the JPG?

Usually not. PNG files can be larger than JPG files because the formats use different compression methods. The result depends on the image content.

Will converting JPG to PNG make the background transparent?

No. JPG does not store transparency. The converted PNG will retain the visible background. You need a separate background-removal or editing tool to make a background transparent.

Are my images uploaded to a server?

This tool uses your browser to read and convert the selected image. The conversion code does not intentionally upload your image to a server.

Which files can I convert?

This tool accepts JPG and JPEG images up to 25 MB per file. Other formats should be converted using a compatible image converter.

Can I use this converter on my phone?

Yes. You can use it in a modern mobile browser. The upload area, preview cards, and controls adapt to smaller screens.
