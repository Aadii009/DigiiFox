Frequently Asked Questions

What does an image compressor do?

An image compressor helps reduce an image's file size. This can make images easier to share and may help websites load faster.

Which image formats are supported?

You can upload JPEG, PNG, and WebP images. You can choose WebP, JPEG, or PNG for the output. Animated images are not supported by this tool.

Will compressing reduce image quality?

Lossy formats such as JPEG and WebP may lose some detail as quality is reduced. You can adjust the slider to balance file size and appearance.

Can I compress a PNG without losing quality?

This browser-based tool can export PNG images, but its quality slider does not control PNG compression. PNG output may be larger than the original. For smaller files, try WebP or JPEG if those formats suit your image.

Are my images uploaded to a server?

No. The image processing takes place in your browser. Your selected image is not sent to a DigiiFox server by this tool.

Why is the compressed image sometimes larger?

The output format, quality setting, image content, and dimensions affect file size. If the output is larger, try a lower quality setting, resize the image, or choose a different format.
