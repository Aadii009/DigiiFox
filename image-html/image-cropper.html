Frequently Asked Questions

Is this image cropper free?

Yes. You can use the DigiiFox Image Cropper for free in your browser.

Which image formats are supported?

You can upload JPG, PNG, and WebP images. Cropped images can be exported as PNG, JPG, or WebP.

How do I crop an image to a square?

Choose the 1:1 — Square option in the Aspect Ratio menu, then position and resize the crop area as needed.

Will cropping reduce my image quality?

Cropping removes pixels outside the selected area. PNG export preserves the cropped pixels without lossy compression; JPG and WebP quality depends on the selected quality setting.

Are my images uploaded to a server?

No. The cropping and image export happen locally in your browser. This page loads the Cropper.js library from a third-party CDN, so an internet connection is required for the editor to initialize.

What is the maximum file size?

Images can be up to 25 MB and 40 megapixels. Very large images may still be limited by your device's available memory.
