What Is Image Resolution?

Image resolution describes the number of pixels in an image, usually expressed as width × height. For example, 1920 × 1080 means the image is 1920 pixels wide and 1080 pixels tall.

Increasing pixel dimensions creates a larger image but does not necessarily restore fine details. Reducing dimensions can make images easier to share or use on websites. JPG and WebP quality settings can also affect file size and visual quality.

Frequently Asked Questions

1. Is this image resolution changer free?

Yes. You can use this tool to resize supported images for free in your browser.

2. Will changing resolution improve image quality?

Not necessarily. Increasing dimensions makes the image larger in pixels, but ordinary resizing cannot recreate missing details. For a smaller image, resizing down may help reduce its file size.

3. What does maintain aspect ratio mean?

It keeps the original width-to-height proportions when you change one dimension, helping prevent the image from looking stretched or squashed.

4. Which image formats are supported?

You can upload JPG, JPEG, PNG and WebP images. The output can be JPG, PNG or WebP.

5. Are my images uploaded to a server?

No. Image resizing is performed locally in your browser. Your image is not sent to a DigiiFox server by this tool.

6. Why can't I use very large dimensions?

Large images can use substantial memory and may cause your browser to slow down. This tool limits the output to 12,000 pixels per side and 40 megapixels.
