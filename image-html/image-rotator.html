How to Rotate an Image

  1. Upload your image. Select a JPG, PNG or WebP image from your device.
  2. Rotate or flip. Use Rotate Left, Rotate Right, the angle buttons, or the flip controls.
  3. Choose your format. Select JPG, PNG or WebP. Adjust quality for JPG or WebP if needed.
  4. Preview and download. Click Rotate & Prepare Download, review the result, and download your image.

Why Use an Image Rotator?

An image rotator helps correct sideways photos, adjust image orientation, and create mirrored versions of pictures. Rotating by 90° or 270° swaps the width and height of the image, while a 180° rotation keeps the same dimensions.

Flipping horizontally creates a mirror image from left to right. Flipping vertically reverses the image from top to bottom. Your changes are applied to the exported image when you prepare the download.

Frequently Asked Questions

1. Is this image rotator free?

Yes. You can rotate and flip supported images for free in your browser.

2. Can I rotate an image by 90 degrees?

Yes. Use Rotate Left or Rotate Right, or select a specific angle using the 0°, 90°, 180° and 270° buttons.

3. What is the difference between rotating and flipping?

Rotating turns the image around its center. Flipping mirrors the image horizontally or vertically.

4. Will rotating change the image dimensions?

A 90° or 270° rotation swaps the width and height. A 0° or 180° rotation keeps the same width and height.

5. Which formats can I use?

You can upload JPG, JPEG, PNG or WebP images and export the result as JPG, PNG or WebP.

6. Are my images uploaded to a server?

No. This tool processes images locally in your browser. Your image is not sent to a DigiiFox server by this tool.
