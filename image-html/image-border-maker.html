Frequently Asked Questions

How do I add a border to an image?

Upload your image, choose the border thickness and color, select a style, and download the result.

Can I change the border color?

Yes. Use the color picker or enter a valid hexadecimal color code to customize the border and background.

Can I create rounded corners?

Yes. Adjust the Corner Roundness slider to create rounded corners around your image.

Which image formats are supported?

You can upload JPG, PNG and WebP images. You can export your finished image as PNG, JPG or WebP.

Are my images uploaded to a server?

No. Image processing takes place locally in your browser. This tool does not upload your image to a DigiiFox server.

Will transparency be preserved?

PNG and WebP can preserve transparent areas of the original image, except where the selected background or border is drawn. JPG does not support transparency and uses the selected background color.

Will the border reduce image quality?

The border itself is drawn at the output resolution. PNG is lossless, while JPG and WebP quality settings affect compression.
