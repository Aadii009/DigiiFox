Frequently Asked Questions

Is this WebP to JPG converter free?

Yes. You can convert WebP images to JPG for free using DigiiFox.

Why convert WebP to JPG?

JPG is supported by many image viewers, applications, websites, and older workflows. Converting can help when a particular application does not accept WebP.

What happens to transparent areas?

JPG does not support transparency. Transparent areas in your WebP image are filled with white during conversion.

Will the converted JPG look exactly the same?

Not necessarily. JPG uses lossy compression, which may change image details. A higher quality setting usually preserves more visual detail.

Will the JPG file always be smaller?

No. File size depends on the original WebP, the image content, and the selected JPG quality. The converted file may be larger or smaller.

Are my images uploaded to a server?

No. Conversion is performed locally in your browser. This tool does not send your selected image to a DigiiFox server.

What is the maximum supported image size?

The tool accepts WebP files up to 25 MB and 40 megapixels. Very large images may require more memory than some devices can provide.
