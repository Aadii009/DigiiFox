Frequently Asked Questions

What does brightness do?

Brightness changes how light or dark the image appears. Increasing it can help reveal details in dark photos, while reducing it can make an image darker.

What does contrast do?

Contrast changes the difference between lighter and darker areas. Higher contrast can make an image look more defined, while lower contrast creates a flatter appearance.

Will my original image be changed?

No. The tool edits a browser preview and exports a new image. Your original file remains unchanged unless you choose to replace it yourself.

Are my images uploaded to a server?

No. Image processing happens locally in your browser. This tool does not upload your selected image to a DigiiFox server.

Which formats can I download?

You can export PNG, JPG, or WebP. The available input formats depend on the image formats supported by your browser.

Will adjusting brightness improve a blurry photo?

No. Brightness and contrast can improve the appearance of lighting and tonal differences, but they cannot restore details that are missing because of blur.
