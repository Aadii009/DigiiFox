Frequently Asked Questions

What does an image upscaler do?

An image upscaler increases an image's pixel dimensions. This version uses browser canvas resizing rather than an AI model.

What is the difference between 2× and 4×?

2× doubles the width and height, producing four times as many pixels. 4× quadruples the width and height, producing sixteen times as many pixels.

Does this tool use AI?

No. This version enlarges images using browser canvas interpolation. It does not generate new details like an AI-powered upscaler.

Will my image become sharper?

The image will have more pixels, but enlargement cannot recover details that were not present in the original. A larger image may still look soft.

Will transparency be preserved?

Choose PNG or WebP to preserve transparency where supported. JPG does not support transparency, so transparent areas become white.

Are my images uploaded to a server?

No. The resizing happens locally in your browser, and the tool does not send your image to DigiiFox.

Why can't I upscale some very large images?

Large output dimensions can require substantial memory. This tool limits the output to 40 megapixels and 12,000 pixels on either side to reduce browser failures.
