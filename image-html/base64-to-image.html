How to Use Base64 to Image

  1. Copy your Base64 string or complete Data URL.
  2. Paste it into the input box.
  3. Click Decode Image and wait for the preview.
  4. Review the image format, dimensions, and decoded size.
  5. Choose the original format, PNG, JPG, or WebP.
  6. Click Download Image to save the result.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is Base64 to Image conversion?

It decodes text that represents binary image data and turns it back into image content that your browser can display.

Can I paste a complete Data URL?

Yes. A Data URL such as data:image/png;base64,... includes the image MIME type and encoded data. You can also paste a raw Base64 string.

Which image formats are supported?

The tool supports common browser-decodable image formats. PNG, JPEG, and WebP are the main download options. Support for other formats depends on your browser.

Are my Base64 data and images uploaded?

The decoding and image conversion happen in your browser. The tool does not intentionally upload your pasted data or decoded image to a DigiiFox server.

Why does my Base64 string fail?

The text may be incomplete, contain invalid characters, use an unsupported format, or exceed the size limit. Make sure you copy the entire encoded string.

Will converting to JPG preserve transparency?

No. JPG does not support transparency. Transparent areas are filled with white when exporting as JPG.
