Frequently Asked Questions

Can I convert multiple images into one PDF?

Yes. Add multiple images and the tool places each image on its own PDF page in the order shown in the image list.

Can I change the order of the pages?

Yes. Use the up and down arrows beside an image to change its position before generating the PDF.

What does Fit to Image mean?

Fit to Image creates a PDF page whose dimensions follow each image's aspect ratio and dimensions. A small margin, if selected, is applied around the image.

Will my image quality be reduced?

The quality setting controls JPEG compression for the images embedded in the PDF. Higher quality usually produces a larger PDF. Some input formats are converted to JPEG during processing.

Are my files uploaded to a server?

The conversion is performed in your browser, without intentionally uploading your images to a DigiiFox server. The PDF library is loaded from a third-party CDN, so an internet connection is required unless you host the library locally.

Can I convert transparent PNG images?

Yes. Transparent areas are rendered using the selected page background before the image is added to the PDF.
