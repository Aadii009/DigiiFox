Which Image Format Should You Choose?

JPG

JPEG / JPG

Ideal for photographs and everyday images. Usually produces compact files, but does not support transparency.

PNG

Portable Network Graphics

Ideal for logos, screenshots, graphics, and images that need transparent backgrounds.

WEBP

WebP

A modern web image format that supports lossy and lossless compression and transparency.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is this image format converter free?

Yes. You can convert supported images between JPG, PNG, and WebP without paying for the conversion tool.

Can I convert PNG to JPG?

Yes. Upload a PNG and choose JPG as the output format. Transparent areas will be filled with white because JPG does not support transparency.

Can I convert JPG to PNG?

Yes. Choose PNG as the output format. Converting to PNG does not restore details or quality already lost in the original JPG.

Will image transparency be preserved?

PNG and WebP can preserve transparency. JPG cannot, so transparent pixels are filled with white when converting to JPG.

Does converting an image improve its quality?

Not necessarily. Converting formats does not restore lost image detail. JPG and WebP quality settings affect compression and file size.

Are my images uploaded to a server?

No. This tool uses your browser to process images. Your selected image is not uploaded to DigiiFox servers.

Why is the converted image larger?

Different formats use different compression methods. PNG files are often larger than JPG or WebP, especially for photographs.
