Frequently Asked Questions

How does this background color changer work?

You select an existing background color from your image. The tool identifies pixels with similar colors and replaces them with your chosen color. You can restrict replacement to connected areas or select matching colors throughout the image.

Does it automatically remove any background?

No. This is a color-based replacement tool, not AI background removal. It works best with plain or relatively uniform backgrounds. Complex backgrounds, hair, transparent objects, and similar-colored subjects may require specialized editing.

What does color tolerance mean?

Tolerance controls how different a pixel can be from the selected background color and still be selected. A low value is more precise; a higher value selects a wider range of shades.

What does connected background mode do?

It replaces matching pixels connected to the point you selected. This helps preserve similar-colored areas elsewhere in the image, such as a white shirt against a white wall.

Which download formats are supported?

You can export PNG, JPG, or WebP. PNG is generally a good choice for clean edges. JPG does not support transparency, although this tool exports the selected replacement color as part of the image.

Are my images uploaded to a server?

No. The image processing runs locally in your browser. The selected image is not uploaded to a DigiiFox server by this tool.
