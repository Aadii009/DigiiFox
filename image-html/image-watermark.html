How to Add a Watermark to an Image

  1. Upload an image. Choose a JPG, PNG or WebP file from your device.
  2. Enter your watermark. Type your brand name, signature or copyright notice.
  3. Customize its appearance. Choose the font, size, color, opacity, rotation and position.
  4. Choose an output format. Select JPG, PNG or WebP. Adjust quality for JPG or WebP.
  5. Download your image. Apply the watermark and save the finished image to your device.

Why Use an Image Watermark?

A watermark can help identify the creator of an image, display a business or photography brand, and communicate copyright information. A subtle watermark can protect your branding without distracting too much from the picture.

Watermarks are not foolproof copyright protection: they may be cropped out or removed. Keep an original copy of your image, and consider placing the watermark where it is harder to crop away.

Frequently Asked Questions

1. Is this image watermark tool free?

Yes. You can add a text watermark to supported images and download the result for free.

2. Can I change the watermark color and transparency?

Yes. Choose a text color and adjust the opacity slider from 10% to 100%.

3. Can I move the watermark?

Yes. Choose from nine positions, including the corners, edges and center of the image.

4. Will the original image quality be preserved?

The tool exports at the original image dimensions. The final file size and quality depend on the chosen format and quality setting. JPG does not support transparency.

5. Are my images uploaded to a server?

No. This tool uses your browser's local image-processing features; it does not send your image to a DigiiFox server.

6. Can I add an image or logo as a watermark?

This version supports text watermarks. Logo and image watermarks are not included in this version.
