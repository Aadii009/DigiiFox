How to Use OCR PDF

  1. Upload your scanned PDF document.
  2. Choose whether to process all pages or a single page.
  3. Select the language used in the document.
  4. Click Start OCR and wait for recognition to finish.
  5. Review the recognized text, copy it, or download it as a TXT file.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

What does OCR PDF do?

OCR means Optical Character Recognition. It attempts to identify text inside scanned PDF pages and images so that the text can be copied or saved.

Can I extract text from a scanned PDF?

Yes. This tool renders PDF pages as images and uses OCR to recognize visible text. Results depend on image quality, handwriting, language, and page layout.

Are my PDF files uploaded to a server?

This page processes PDF pages in your browser. OCR libraries and language data are loaded from external CDNs, but the document is not intentionally uploaded by this tool. Your browser and network environment may still affect privacy.

Why does OCR take so long?

OCR analyzes page images pixel by pixel. Large documents, high-resolution pages, and multiple languages can take longer to process.

Will the extracted text be perfectly accurate?

Not always. Blurry scans, unusual fonts, tables, columns, and handwritten text can cause recognition errors. Always review important results.

Which languages are supported?

The current version offers English, Hindi, English plus Hindi, French, German, and Spanish. Language data must be available online when needed.

Can I download the recognized text?

Yes. You can copy the results to your clipboard or download them as a plain-text TXT file.
