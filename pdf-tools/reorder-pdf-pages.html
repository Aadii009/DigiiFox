NEED HELP?

Frequently Asked Questions

What does a PDF page organizer do?

It lets you change the order of pages in a PDF and remove pages you no longer need. You can then download a new PDF with those changes.

How do I move a page?

Use the up arrow to move a page earlier in the document and the down arrow to move it later. The first page cannot move up, and the last page cannot move down.

Can I delete multiple pages?

Yes. Click Remove on each page you want to exclude. You must keep at least one page in the final PDF.

Will my original PDF be changed?

No. The organizer downloads a new PDF. Your original file remains unchanged on your device.

Is my PDF uploaded to a server?

No. The document is processed in your browser and is not uploaded to a DigiiFox server. The PDF processing libraries are loaded from third-party CDNs, so an internet connection is needed to load them.

Why can't I open my PDF?

The file may be damaged, encrypted, or not a valid PDF. Try opening it in a PDF reader and saving an accessible, unencrypted copy before uploading it again.

Does organizing a PDF reduce its file size?

This tool changes the page order and removes selected pages. It does not specifically compress the remaining pages or guarantee a smaller file.

Is the PDF Organizer free?

Yes. You can use this tool to organize PDF pages without paying for this feature.
