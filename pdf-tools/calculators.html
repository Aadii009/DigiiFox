Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers about using our calculators.

Are DigiiFox calculators free to use?

Yes. The calculators in this collection are intended to be available for free in your browser.

Are the results always exact?

Results depend on the inputs, formula and assumptions used. Some financial and health calculators provide estimates. Review the explanation on each individual calculator.

Do I need to install anything?

No installation is required for browser-based calculators. Open the relevant page in a modern web browser.

Are my calculations uploaded to a server?

The initial calculators are designed to calculate in your browser. Avoid entering sensitive information into an online tool unless its data-handling practices are clear.

Can I use these calculators on my phone?

Yes. The page is designed to adapt to desktop, tablet and mobile screens.

Can I rely on a calculator for tax, investment or medical decisions?

Use such results as general information only. Check applicable official guidance and consult a qualified professional when personalized advice is needed.
