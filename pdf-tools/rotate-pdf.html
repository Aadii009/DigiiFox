Frequently asked questions

Can I rotate only one page?

Yes. Select "Selected pages" and enter the page number, such as 3.

Can I rotate multiple pages?

Yes. Enter numbers separated by commas, such as 1, 3, 5, or use ranges such as 2-6.

Is this PDF rotator free?

Yes. This version of the DigiiFox PDF rotator is free to use.

Are my files uploaded?

No. The tool processes your PDF locally in your browser. The PDF library is downloaded from a third-party CDN.
