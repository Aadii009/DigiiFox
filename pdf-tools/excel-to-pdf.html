Frequently Asked Questions

Can I convert XLS and XLSX files?

Yes. This tool supports common Excel XLS and XLSX files.

Can I select a specific worksheet?

Yes. Upload your workbook and choose the worksheet you want to convert.

Will formulas and charts work in the PDF?

The converter exports the spreadsheet's available cell values. It does not recalculate formulas or reproduce Excel charts, macros, or all advanced formatting.

Can I convert the whole workbook into one PDF?

This version exports one selected worksheet at a time. Select another worksheet and convert it separately if needed.

Are my files uploaded to a server?

The spreadsheet is processed locally in your browser by this page. The conversion libraries are loaded from external CDNs, so an internet connection is required.
