Your privacy matters

Your PDF is processed in your browser. DigiiFox does not upload the document to its own server. Required PDF libraries load from third-party CDNs, so an internet connection is needed.

How to Use Crop PDF

  1. Choose a PDF file from your device.
  2. Preview your pages using the page navigation controls.
  3. Enter the margins you want to remove from the page edges.
  4. Choose whether to crop all pages or only the current page.
  5. Select Crop and Download PDF to save your result.

Frequently Asked Questions

What does cropping a PDF do?

Cropping changes the visible page area to hide unwanted margins or surrounding content.

Does cropping permanently erase content?

No. Standard PDF cropping changes the visible page boundaries. Content outside those boundaries may remain in the file.

What measurement does this tool use?

Margins are measured in PDF points. There are 72 points in one inch.

Can I crop just one page?

Yes. Select Current page only to crop the page currently displayed in the preview.

Is my PDF uploaded?

No. Processing happens locally in your browser, although the required libraries load from external CDNs.
