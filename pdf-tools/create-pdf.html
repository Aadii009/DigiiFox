How to Use Create PDF

  1. Enter a title for your document.
  2. Write or paste your text into the editor.
  3. Use the toolbar to add bold text, headings, or lists.
  4. Select your preferred paper size and orientation.
  5. Check the document preview.
  6. Click Download PDF to save your document.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

What does the Create PDF tool do?

It converts the text and basic formatting you enter into a downloadable PDF document.

Can I format my document?

Yes. You can use bold, italic, underline, headings, subheadings, and ordered or unordered lists.

Can I choose the PDF page size?

Yes. You can choose A4, Letter, or Legal paper and select portrait or landscape orientation.

Are my documents uploaded to a server?

This tool generates the PDF in your browser and does not intentionally upload your document. The PDF library is downloaded from an external CDN.

Can I create a multi-page PDF?

Yes. Longer documents can flow onto multiple pages. Page breaks and spacing may vary depending on the content and browser rendering.

Can I convert a Word file with this tool?

This version creates PDFs from text entered or pasted into the editor. It does not directly open Word documents.

Why is the downloaded PDF slightly different from the preview?

PDF generation uses browser-based HTML rendering. Fonts, page breaks, and spacing may differ slightly between the preview and the final PDF.
