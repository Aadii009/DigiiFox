Frequently Asked Questions

Can I extract specific PDF pages?

Yes. Enter page numbers such as 1, 3, 5-8 to create a new PDF containing those pages in the requested order.

Can I split every page into its own PDF?

Yes. Choose Individual Pages to create one PDF per page, packaged into a ZIP archive.

Are my PDFs uploaded to a server?

The code processes your PDF locally and does not transmit its contents to a DigiiFox backend. The page does download its processing libraries from a CDN.
