How to Use Delete PDF Pages

  1. Click Choose PDF and upload your document.
  2. Wait for the page previews to appear.
  3. Click the checkbox on each page you want to remove.
  4. Review the selected-page count before proceeding.
  5. Click Delete Selected Pages & Download PDF.
  6. Your updated PDF will be downloaded automatically.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Can I delete multiple pages at once?

Yes. Select any number of pages, or use Select All. You must leave at least one page in the PDF.

Will the remaining pages stay in order?

Yes. The remaining pages keep their original order.

Will the original PDF be changed?

No. This tool creates a separate PDF download. Your original file remains unchanged.

Is my PDF uploaded to a server?

No. This page processes your document locally in your browser. The PDF libraries are loaded from external CDNs, so an internet connection is required.

Why can't I delete every page?

A valid PDF document must contain at least one page. Select fewer pages if every page is currently selected.

Can I use this tool with a scanned PDF?

Yes. You can remove pages from scanned PDFs too, because this tool removes PDF pages rather than extracting their text.
