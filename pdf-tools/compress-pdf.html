Frequently Asked Questions

Will every PDF become smaller?

No. The result depends on the document's existing structure. Some files may become smaller, while others may remain similar in size or become larger.

Does this version reduce image quality?

This initial version does not intentionally recompress embedded images. Image-based PDFs may therefore see little or no reduction in size.

Are my files uploaded?

The processing code works in your browser and does not send PDF contents to a DigiiFox backend. The browser does load the PDF library from a CDN.
