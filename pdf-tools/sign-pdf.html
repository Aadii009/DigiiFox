Frequently Asked Questions

Is this a legally valid digital signature?

This tool adds a visual signature image to a PDF. It does not create a certificate-based digital signature or independently verify your identity. Legal requirements vary by document and jurisdiction.

Can I sign a specific page?

Yes. Use the page selector to choose a page, then position your signature on the preview before downloading.

Can I change the signature size and color?

Yes. Choose an ink color before drawing, then use the signature size slider to resize the signature on the PDF page.

Will the original PDF text remain selectable?

Yes, the tool adds the signature image to the existing PDF rather than converting every page into an image. Existing document features may still vary depending on the PDF.

Is my document uploaded to a server?

No. PDF processing happens in your browser. The required JavaScript libraries load from CDNs, so an internet connection is needed to open this tool.
