Frequently asked questions

Can I convert selected PDF pages?

Yes. Select "Selected pages" and enter page numbers such as 1, 3, 5-7.

Can I download all JPG images together?

Yes. Use the Download ZIP button to download all converted images in one archive.

Does converting PDF to JPG change the original PDF?

No. The converter creates new JPG images and leaves your original PDF unchanged.

Are my files uploaded to DigiiFox?

No. PDF rendering and JPG creation happen locally in your browser. The libraries used by this page are loaded from third-party CDNs.
