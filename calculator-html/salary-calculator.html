UNDERSTANDING SALARY

What is a Salary Calculator?

A salary calculator estimates how much of your annual compensation may reach you after employer-side CTC components, employee payroll deductions, and income tax are accounted for.

Your actual take-home pay depends on your salary structure, benefits, provident fund arrangements, state-specific professional tax, tax regime, and employer payroll policies.

SALARY FORMULA

How is take-home salary calculated?

Step 1: Gross cash salary

Gross cash salary = CTC − employer PF − gratuity − other employer benefits

Step 2: Total deductions

Total deductions = employee PF + professional tax + other payroll deductions + entered income tax

Step 3: Annual take-home

Annual take-home = gross cash salary − total deductions

Step 4: Monthly estimate

Average monthly take-home = annual take-home ÷ 12

IMPORTANT DISTINCTIONS

CTC vs Gross Salary vs Take-Home

CTC

The total annual cost of employment, which may include cash salary, employer contributions, benefits, and other components.

Gross cash salary

The cash salary estimate after removing the employer contributions and benefits entered as part of CTC.

Take-home salary

The estimated amount remaining after the payroll deductions and income tax entered in the calculator.

COMMON QUESTIONS

Frequently Asked Questions

Learn more about salary, CTC, and take-home pay.

Is CTC the same as take-home salary?

No. CTC can include employer contributions and benefits that are not paid directly as monthly cash. Employee deductions and income tax can reduce take-home pay further.

Does this calculator automatically calculate income tax?

No. Enter your estimated annual income tax or TDS in the provided field. For a separate tax estimate, use the DigiiFox Income Tax Calculator and enter the result here.

Is variable pay included in the monthly estimate?

Variable pay is included in the annual gross cash salary when you enter it as part of CTC. The calculator also shows a fixed monthly take-home estimate that excludes the variable pay from fixed salary while allocating the entered annual deductions and tax across the year.

Does the calculator include employer PF?

Yes. Enter the employer PF contribution if it is included in your CTC. The calculator removes that amount from CTC to estimate gross cash salary. Employee PF is entered separately as a payroll deduction.

Why can my actual payslip be different?

Actual payroll may use a different salary structure, variable-pay schedule, tax withholding, PF calculation, professional tax, benefit valuation, or rounding method.

Can I use this calculator outside India?

The calculator uses rupees and an Indian-style salary structure. For another country, adapt the currency, deductions, payroll rules, and tax calculations to that jurisdiction.
