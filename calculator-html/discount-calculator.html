UNDERSTAND THE TOOL

What Is a Discount Calculator?

A discount calculator helps you determine how much money you save when a product or service is offered at a reduced price. It calculates the discount amount and the final price you pay.

For example, if an item costs ₹2,000 and a seller offers a 20% discount, you save ₹400 and pay ₹1,600.

A discount calculator can also find the discount percentage from two prices, calculate successive discounts, and estimate the original price from a discounted price.

MATHEMATICAL BASICS

Discount Formulas

These formulas explain how each calculation works.

₹

Discount Amount

Original Price × Discount % ÷ 100

Example: ₹2,000 × 20 ÷ 100 = ₹400 saved.

=

Final Sale Price

Original Price − Discount Amount

Example: ₹2,000 − ₹400 = ₹1,600.

%

Discount Percentage

(Original − Sale) ÷ Original × 100

Example: (₹2,000 − ₹1,500) ÷ ₹2,000 × 100 = 25%.

↶

Original Price

Sale Price ÷ (1 − Discount % ÷ 100)

Example: ₹1,600 ÷ 0.80 = ₹2,000.

+

Two Successive Discounts

Final = Original × (1 − A/100) × (1 − B/100)

Example: ₹1,000 with 20%, then 10% off = ₹720.

✓

Total Savings

Original Price − Final Price

Example: ₹1,000 − ₹720 = ₹280 saved.

Important: Successive discounts are not simply added together. A 20% discount followed by an additional 10% discount gives a total reduction of 28%, not 30%.

Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions about discounts and sale prices.

How do I calculate a discount?

Multiply the original price by the discount percentage and divide by 100. Subtract the discount amount from the original price to find the final price.

How much do I save on a ₹1,000 item with 30% off?

You save ₹300 because ₹1,000 × 30 ÷ 100 = ₹300. The final price is ₹700.

How do I find the original price after a discount?

Divide the sale price by one minus the discount rate expressed as a decimal. For example, ₹1,600 after 20% off means ₹1,600 ÷ 0.80 = ₹2,000.

Do two discounts of 20% and 10% equal 30% off?

No. The second discount is applied to the already reduced price. The combined discount is 28%: 20% off ₹1,000 leaves ₹800, and another 10% off leaves ₹720.

Can a discount be more than 100%?

A conventional retail discount cannot reduce a positive price by more than 100% without requiring additional payment or credit. This calculator limits standard percentage discounts to 0–100%.

Does this calculator include GST or shipping?

No. It calculates the discount and sale price based on the price you enter. Taxes, shipping, service charges, and other fees are not included.

Is the Discount Calculator free?

Yes. It is free to use and performs its calculations in your browser without requiring an account.
