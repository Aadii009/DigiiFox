What Is a Recurring Deposit Calculator?

A Recurring Deposit (RD) calculator estimates how much you may accumulate by depositing a fixed amount regularly, usually every month, for a selected period. It estimates the total contributions, interest earned, and potential maturity amount.

RD products and interest conventions differ across financial institutions. This calculator uses a simplified monthly-growth model, so its estimate may not exactly match a bank's official maturity quote.

RD Calculator Formula

Since deposits are made regularly, each instalment earns interest for a different length of time. This calculator models each end-of-month instalment separately.

i = (1 + r/n)n/12 − 1

Then the estimated maturity amount is:

M = P × Σ (1 + i)k

Here, the sum runs from k = 0 to N − 1, where N is the total number of monthly deposits.

  • M = Estimated maturity amount
  • P = Monthly deposit amount
  • r = Annual interest rate expressed as a decimal
  • n = Selected compounding periods per year
  • i = Effective monthly growth rate
  • N = Total number of monthly deposits

Total deposits = Monthly deposit × Number of deposits.

Estimated interest = Maturity amount − Total deposits.

Frequently Asked Questions

How is an RD different from an FD?

An RD generally involves regular instalments over time. An FD generally involves placing a lump sum for a fixed term.

Is the maturity amount guaranteed by this calculator?

No. This tool provides an estimate. Your financial institution's applicable rate, deposit schedule, calculation rules, and terms determine the actual maturity value.

Does the calculator include taxes or penalties?

No. The estimate excludes taxes, TDS, late-payment charges, premature-closure penalties, and other bank-specific charges.

What happens if I change the compounding frequency?

The calculator derives an effective monthly growth rate from the selected annual rate and compounding frequency. A different frequency may therefore change the estimated maturity amount.

Does the currency selector convert money?

No. It changes the displayed currency symbol and number formatting only. It does not perform foreign-exchange conversion.
