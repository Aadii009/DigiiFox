UNDERSTAND THE BASICS

What Is an EMI Calculator?

EMI stands for Equated Monthly Installment. It is the regular monthly payment a borrower makes to repay a loan over an agreed period. For a fixed-rate loan, the EMI generally includes both principal repayment and interest.

ƒ(x)

EMI Formula

EMI = P × r × (1 + r)n ÷ [(1 + r)n − 1]

When the monthly interest rate is zero, EMI = P ÷ n.

P

Principal (P)

The amount borrowed before interest and additional charges.

r

Monthly Interest Rate

The annual interest rate divided by 12 and converted into a decimal.

r = Annual Rate ÷ 1200
n

Number of Payments

The total number of monthly installments over the loan tenure.

n = Years × 12

COMMON QUESTIONS

Frequently Asked Questions

Understand how monthly loan repayments work.

What does EMI stand for?

EMI stands for Equated Monthly Installment. It is the regular monthly payment made toward a loan, generally covering both principal and interest.

How is monthly EMI calculated?

EMI is calculated using the principal amount, monthly interest rate, and total number of monthly installments. For a positive fixed interest rate, the standard formula is P × r × (1 + r)^n ÷ ((1 + r)^n − 1).

Does a longer loan tenure reduce EMI?

Generally, yes. A longer tenure spreads the repayment over more months, reducing the monthly installment. However, it usually increases the total interest paid over the life of the loan.

Does a lower interest rate reduce EMI?

Generally, yes. For the same principal and tenure, a lower fixed interest rate results in a lower EMI and usually less total interest.

Can I calculate EMI for a zero-interest loan?

Yes. When the interest rate is zero, the calculator divides the principal equally across the number of monthly payments.

Does the calculator include processing fees?

No. This calculator estimates principal and interest repayments. Processing fees, insurance, taxes, late-payment charges, and other lender costs are not included.

Is the EMI result guaranteed by my lender?

No. This is an estimate based on the details you enter. Your lender may use different rounding rules, dates, fees, or repayment conditions.

Is my loan information uploaded?

No. Calculations are performed in your browser. The calculator does not send your loan details to a server.
