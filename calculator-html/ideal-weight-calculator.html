What Is an Ideal Weight Calculator?

An ideal weight calculator estimates a reference weight from a person's height using mathematical formulas developed for adults. Different formulas produce different results, so this calculator displays several estimates rather than presenting one number as universally ideal.

Formulas Used

Each formula starts with a reference weight at 5 feet (60 inches) and adjusts for each additional inch of height.

Robinson (1983)

Female: 49 kg + 1.7 kg per inch above 5 ft.

Miller (1983)

Female: 53.1 kg + 1.36 kg per inch above 5 ft.

Devine (1974)

Female: 45.5 kg + 2.3 kg per inch above 5 ft.

Hamwi (1964)

Female: 45.5 kg + 2.2 kg per inch above 5 ft.

How to Use This Calculator

Frequently Asked Questions

Is there one perfect weight for everyone?

No. Weight alone cannot determine health. Body composition, age, medical history, and other factors also matter.

Why do the formulas give different results?

Each formula was developed using different assumptions and reference values, so their estimates can differ.

Can I use this calculator for children?

No. These formulas are intended as adult reference estimates. Children and teenagers need age- and sex-specific growth assessments.

Does this calculator tell me whether I am healthy?

No. It estimates reference weights from height; it does not assess your current weight, body composition, or overall health.

Are my measurements uploaded?

No. The calculations run in your browser. This page's calculator code does not send your measurements to a server.

Health disclaimer: This tool is for general educational purposes only and is not medical advice. Consult a qualified healthcare professional for personalised guidance, especially if you are pregnant, under 18, managing a medical condition, or concerned about changes in your weight.
