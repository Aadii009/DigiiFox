OVERVIEW

What Is a Weight Converter?

A weight converter changes a measurement from one mass unit to another. For example, you can convert kilograms to pounds, grams to ounces, or metric tonnes to kilograms.

These conversions are useful for cooking, shipping, shopping, science, education, fitness, and many everyday activities. Although people commonly say "weight converter", most everyday conversions between kilograms, grams, pounds, and ounces are technically conversions of mass.

THE MATHEMATICS

Weight Conversion Formula

This converter uses kilograms as its common base unit. First, convert the original value to kilograms. Then divide by the target unit's kilogram factor.

Step 1

Convert to kilograms

Kilograms = Value × Source factor
Step 2

Convert to target unit

Result = Kilograms ÷ Target factor

Example: Convert 5 kilograms to grams

1 kilogram = 1,000 grams

5 × 1,000 = 5,000 g

Therefore, 5 kilograms equals 5,000 grams.
