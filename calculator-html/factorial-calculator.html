    UNDERSTANDING THE MATH

    What is a factorial?

    A factorial is a mathematical operation that multiplies a non-negative integer by every positive integer smaller than it, down to 1. The factorial of a number n is written as n!.

    Factorials are used in permutations, combinations, probability, statistics, algebra, and many other areas of mathematics.

    Standard factorial formula n! = n × (n − 1) × (n − 2) × ... × 2 × 1
    0!

    Zero factorial

    Zero factorial is defined as 1. This convention is essential for many mathematical formulas.

    0! = 1
    1!

    One factorial

    The factorial of 1 is 1 because there is only one positive integer in the product.

    1! = 1
    5!

    Example factorial

    Multiply all positive integers from 5 down to 1 to calculate the factorial of 5.

    5! = 5 × 4 × 3 × 2 × 1 = 120

    Important factorial rules

    General formula n! = n × (n − 1)!
    Zero factorial 0! = 1
    One factorial 1! = 1
    Example 6! = 720

    COMMON QUESTIONS

    Frequently asked questions

    What is the factorial of 0?

    The factorial of 0 is 1. This is a mathematical definition that makes factorial formulas consistent in combinations and other calculations.

    Can a factorial be calculated for a negative number?

    The ordinary factorial operation is defined for non-negative integers. This calculator does not accept negative integers or fractional inputs.

    Why do factorials become so large?

    Each new factorial multiplies the previous factorial by the next integer. As a result, factorial values grow very quickly.

    What is the difference between n and n!?

    The symbol n represents a number, while n! represents the product of all positive integers from 1 through n. For example, 5 is the number, but 5! equals 120.

    Where are factorials used?

    Factorials are commonly used in permutations, combinations, probability, statistics, and mathematical series.

    Does this calculator give exact results?

    Yes. It uses JavaScript BigInt arithmetic to calculate exact integer factorials within the supported range, avoiding floating-point rounding errors.

    Are my calculations processed online?

    The factorial calculation runs locally in your browser. The calculator does not need to upload your entered number to calculate the result.
