UNDERSTAND THE TOOL

What is a Standard Calculator?

A standard calculator is a basic mathematical tool used to perform everyday arithmetic operations. It helps you add, subtract, multiply, and divide numbers, as well as work with percentages, decimals, and grouped expressions.

You can use it for shopping calculations, budgeting, schoolwork, quick estimates, and other everyday tasks.

MATHEMATICAL BASICS

Formulas and Examples

A standard calculator applies the rules of arithmetic to calculate the result of an expression.

+

Addition

a + b = result

Example: 12 + 8 = 20

−

Subtraction

a − b = result

Example: 15 − 6 = 9

×

Multiplication

a × b = result

Example: 7 × 4 = 28

÷

Division

a ÷ b = result

Example: 20 ÷ 5 = 4

%

Percentage

a × b ÷ 100

Example: 200 × 10 ÷ 100 = 20

( )

Parentheses

(a + b) × c

Example: (2 + 3) × 4 = 20

Remember: Multiplication and division are performed before addition and subtraction unless parentheses change the order.

Frequently Asked Questions

Answers to common questions about this calculator.

Is the DigiiFox Standard Calculator free?

Yes. You can use this calculator for everyday arithmetic without paying or creating an account.

Which calculations does it support?

It supports addition, subtraction, multiplication, division, decimals, percentages, and parentheses.

Can I use my computer keyboard?

Yes. You can enter numbers and operators from your keyboard. Use Enter or the equals key to calculate, Backspace to delete, and Escape to clear.

How does the percentage button work?

The percentage button converts the number directly before it into its decimal equivalent. For example, 25% becomes 0.25. You can then use it in an expression.

Why can't I divide by zero?

Division by zero is undefined in ordinary arithmetic. The calculator will show an error if an expression attempts to divide by zero.

Are my calculations uploaded to a server?

No. This calculator performs calculations locally in your browser and does not send your expressions to a calculation server.
