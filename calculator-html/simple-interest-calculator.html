UNDERSTAND THE MATH

Simple Interest Formula

Simple interest is calculated only on the original principal amount. Interest previously earned or charged is not added to the principal for future interest calculations.

Simple Interest SI = (P × R × T) ÷ 100
Total Amount A = P + SI
  • SI: Simple interest
  • P: Original principal amount
  • R: Annual interest rate in percent
  • T: Time in years
  • A: Principal plus simple interest
WORKED EXAMPLE

Example: ₹10,000 at 5%

Suppose you invest ₹10,000 at a simple annual interest rate of 5% for 3 years.

Principal ₹10,000
Annual rate 5%
Duration 3 years
SI = (10,000 × 5 × 3) ÷ 100 = ₹1,500
Total Amount ₹11,500

COMMON QUESTIONS

Frequently Asked Questions

Learn more about simple interest and how it works.

What is simple interest?

Simple interest is interest calculated only on the original principal amount over a specified period. It does not include interest on previously accrued interest.

How is simple interest different from compound interest?

Simple interest is based on the original principal. Compound interest is calculated on the principal plus accumulated interest from earlier periods.

Can I calculate interest for months or days?

Yes. Select months or days in the time-period field. Months are converted using 12 months per year, and days using 365 days per year. Financial institutions may use different day-count conventions.

Can the interest rate be zero?

Yes. If the annual interest rate is zero, the simple interest will also be zero and the total amount will equal the principal.

Does this calculator provide financial advice?

No. It provides a mathematical estimate based on the figures entered. It does not account for taxes, charges, changing rates, or the specific terms of a financial product.
