CONVERSION FORMULAS

Temperature Conversion Formulas

Learn how each temperature scale is converted.

Celsius to Fahrenheit

°F = (°C × 9/5) + 32

Multiply Celsius by 1.8, then add 32.

Fahrenheit to Celsius

°C = (°F − 32) × 5/9

Subtract 32 from Fahrenheit, then multiply by 5/9.

Celsius to Kelvin

K = °C + 273.15

Add 273.15 to the Celsius temperature.

Kelvin to Celsius

°C = K − 273.15

Subtract 273.15 from Kelvin.

Fahrenheit to Kelvin

K = (°F − 32) × 5/9 + 273.15

Convert Fahrenheit to Celsius, then add 273.15.

Kelvin to Fahrenheit

°F = (K − 273.15) × 9/5 + 32

Convert Kelvin to Celsius, then to Fahrenheit.

FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

What is a temperature converter?

A temperature converter changes a temperature between scales such as Celsius, Fahrenheit, and Kelvin.

What is the difference between Celsius and Fahrenheit?

They are different temperature scales with different reference points and interval sizes. Water freezes at 0 °C or 32 °F under standard atmospheric pressure.

Why does Kelvin not use a degree symbol?

Kelvin is an absolute thermodynamic temperature scale. Its unit is written as K, not °K.

Can I enter negative temperatures?

Yes. Negative Celsius and Fahrenheit values are valid as long as the temperature is not below absolute zero. Kelvin values cannot be negative.

Does the converter upload my data?

No. This converter performs calculations directly in your browser and does not need to upload your values.
