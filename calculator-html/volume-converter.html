FORMULAS

Volume Conversion Formulas

Learn how common metric and US customary volume units relate.

Litres to millilitres

mL = L × 1,000

Multiply litres by 1,000 to obtain millilitres.

Millilitres to litres

L = mL ÷ 1,000

Divide millilitres by 1,000 to obtain litres.

Cubic metres to litres

L = m³ × 1,000

One cubic metre equals exactly 1,000 litres.

US gallons to litres

L = US gal × 3.785411784

Multiply US gallons by the standard US gallon factor.

Cubic inches to cubic centimetres

cm³ = in³ × 16.387064

Use this conversion for engine displacement and other volume measurements.

Cubic feet to cubic metres

m³ = ft³ × 0.028316846592

Useful for storage spaces, rooms, and larger volume calculations.

FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

What is a volume converter?

A volume converter changes a measurement from one volume unit to another, such as litres to gallons or cubic metres to litres.

How many millilitres are in one litre?

One litre equals exactly 1,000 millilitres.

How many litres are in one US gallon?

One US liquid gallon equals exactly 3.785411784 litres.

Are US and UK gallons the same?

No. A US liquid gallon is approximately 3.785 litres, while an imperial gallon is approximately 4.546 litres. This converter uses US customary liquid units.

What is the difference between volume and capacity?

Volume describes the space occupied by a three-dimensional object or substance. Capacity commonly refers to how much a container can hold. Litres and cubic metres can be used to express both, depending on the context.

Does the converter upload my values?

No. All conversion calculations run directly in your browser. Your entered values do not need to be sent to a server.
