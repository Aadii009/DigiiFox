What Is a SIP Calculator?

A Systematic Investment Plan (SIP) lets you invest a fixed amount at regular intervals, commonly every month. A SIP calculator estimates how those contributions could accumulate over time under an assumed rate of return.

Actual investment returns can fluctuate. This calculator uses a simplified monthly-return model and does not account for taxes, fees, inflation, or market volatility.

SIP Calculator Formula

For a fixed monthly investment made at the end of each month, the estimated future value is calculated using:

FV = P × [((1 + i)n − 1) / i]
  • FV = Estimated future value
  • P = Monthly investment amount
  • i = Monthly rate of return, calculated as annual rate ÷ 12
  • n = Total number of monthly investments

When the return rate is 0%, the formula simplifies to FV = P × n.

If you enter an annual step-up, the calculator increases your monthly contribution at the start of each investment year and calculates the future value of each contribution separately.

Frequently Asked Questions

Are SIP returns guaranteed?

No. SIP is a method of investing, not a guaranteed-return product. Market-linked investment values can rise or fall.

What is an annual SIP step-up?

A step-up increases your monthly contribution each year by a chosen percentage. For example, a 10% step-up increases a ₹5,000 monthly contribution to ₹5,500 in the second year.

Does this calculator include taxes or fees?

No. The result is a simplified estimate before taxes, fees, inflation, and other investment-specific costs.

Does changing the currency convert my investment?

No. The currency selector only changes how the numbers are displayed. It does not perform foreign-exchange conversion.

Why may my actual investment value differ?

Actual returns vary over time. This calculator assumes a steady monthly rate derived from the annual rate entered, which may differ from real market performance and from the conventions used by some financial calculators.
