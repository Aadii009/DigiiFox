UNDERSTANDING GST

What is GST?

GST stands for Goods and Services Tax. In India, it is an indirect tax applied to many goods and services. The applicable rate depends on the product or service and relevant tax rules.

This calculator helps you estimate the tax on an amount. It is intended for quick calculations and does not determine the legally applicable GST rate for a particular transaction.

GST FORMULA

How is GST calculated?

When adding GST

GST Amount = Original Amount × GST Rate ÷ 100

Final Amount = Original Amount + GST Amount

When removing GST

Original Amount = Inclusive Amount × 100 ÷ (100 + GST Rate)

GST Amount = Inclusive Amount − Original Amount

Tax distribution

Intra-state: CGST = SGST = Total GST ÷ 2

Inter-state: IGST = Total GST

COMMON QUESTIONS

Frequently Asked Questions

Learn more about calculating GST.

What does a GST Calculator do?

It calculates the GST amount on a base price or extracts the tax component from a GST-inclusive price. It also shows the resulting amount and a tax breakdown.

How do I add GST to a price?

Multiply the original amount by the GST rate and divide by 100. Add that tax amount to the original price to obtain the final amount.

How do I remove GST from an inclusive price?

Divide the inclusive price by 1 plus the GST rate expressed as a decimal. Subtract the resulting base amount from the inclusive price to find the GST component.

What is the difference between CGST, SGST, and IGST?

For an intra-state supply, CGST and SGST generally represent the central and state components of GST. For an inter-state supply, IGST generally applies. The applicable treatment depends on the transaction and current tax rules.

Does this calculator identify the correct GST rate?

No. You must select the rate yourself. The correct rate depends on the relevant goods or services and applicable rules. Verify it using official tax guidance or with a qualified tax professional.

Does changing the currency convert the amount?

No. The currency selector only changes how the result is displayed. It does not perform currency conversion.
