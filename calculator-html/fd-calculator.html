What Is a Fixed Deposit Calculator?

A Fixed Deposit (FD) calculator estimates the amount you may receive when a fixed-term deposit matures. You enter a deposit amount, an annual interest rate, a tenure, and a compounding frequency to estimate the principal plus accumulated interest.

Banks may use different interest calculation, payout, rounding, and premature-withdrawal rules. This calculator provides a simplified estimate and does not represent a bank's official quote.

FD Calculator Formula

For compound interest, the estimated maturity amount is:

A = P × (1 + r/n)nt
  • A = Maturity amount
  • P = Initial deposit (principal)
  • r = Annual interest rate expressed as a decimal
  • n = Number of compounding periods per year
  • t = Deposit tenure in years

Interest earned = Maturity amount − Initial deposit.

For this calculator, the annual rate is converted to a decimal, and the chosen compounding frequency determines the number of compounding periods. Actual bank conventions may differ, particularly for short or irregular tenures.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is the maturity amount guaranteed by this calculator?

No. This is a mathematical estimate based on your inputs. Your bank's applicable terms and conditions determine the actual interest and maturity amount.

What is compounding frequency?

It is how often accrued interest is added to the deposit balance for further interest calculations. Common frequencies include yearly, half-yearly, quarterly, and monthly.

Does this calculator include tax or TDS?

No. The displayed interest is before taxes, tax deducted at source (TDS), and other applicable charges.

Can I calculate an FD for a few months?

Yes. Choose Months as the tenure unit and enter the number of months. For days, choose Days and enter the number of days. Banks may calculate short-tenure deposits using different rules.

Does changing the currency convert my money?

No. The currency selector changes the display format only. It does not perform foreign-exchange conversion.
