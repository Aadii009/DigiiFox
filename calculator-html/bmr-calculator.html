What Is BMR?

Basal Metabolic Rate (BMR) is an estimate of the energy your body uses to maintain essential functions at rest, such as breathing, circulation and temperature regulation. It is commonly expressed in kilocalories per day (kcal/day).

BMR Formula

This calculator uses the Mifflin–St Jeor equation. Weight is measured in kilograms, height in centimetres, and age in years.

Male equation BMR = (10 × weight) + (6.25 × height) − (5 × age) + 5
Female equation BMR = (10 × weight) + (6.25 × height) − (5 × age) − 161

BMR vs. Daily Calorie Needs

BMR estimates energy use at rest. Total daily energy expenditure (TDEE) estimates energy use across a typical day, including activity. This calculator approximates TDEE by multiplying BMR by an activity factor.

Daily calorie estimate TDEE ≈ BMR × Activity factor

Frequently Asked Questions

Is BMR the same as TDEE?

No. BMR estimates energy used at rest. TDEE includes estimated energy used through daily activity and exercise.

Can I use BMR as my daily calorie target?

BMR alone is not a personalized eating target. Your energy needs depend on activity, goals, health and other individual factors.

Why is this result only an estimate?

Equations estimate energy needs from a few measurements. Actual metabolism can differ because of body composition, health, medications and other factors.

Does exercise change BMR?

Exercise increases energy expenditure. Long-term changes in body composition and other factors can also affect resting energy use.
