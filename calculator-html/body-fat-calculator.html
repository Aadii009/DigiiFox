What Is Body Fat Percentage?

Body fat percentage is the proportion of total body weight made up of fat. The remaining weight includes muscle, bone, organs, water, and other fat-free tissues. It is one measure of body composition, but it cannot independently determine overall health.

Formula Used

This calculator uses the U.S. Navy circumference equations. Measurements are converted to inches before applying the formulas.

Male reference formula

Body fat % = 86.010 × log₁₀(abdomen − neck) − 70.041 × log₁₀(height) + 36.76

Female reference formula

Body fat % = 163.205 × log₁₀(waist + hip − neck) − 97.684 × log₁₀(height) − 78.387

Here, log₁₀ means the base-10 logarithm. All measurements in these equations are in inches. The female equation uses waist, hip, and neck measurements; the male equation uses abdomen, neck, and height.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is this calculator accurate?

It provides an estimate from circumference measurements. Tape placement, measurement technique, and individual body differences can affect the result. It is not equivalent to a direct body composition assessment.

What is a healthy body fat percentage?

There is no single suitable percentage for everyone. Age, sex, individual health, and the reference used all affect interpretation. A healthcare professional can help interpret your result in context.

What is fat-free mass?

Fat-free mass is estimated by subtracting estimated fat mass from total body weight. It includes muscle, bone, organs, water, and other non-fat tissues.

Can I use this calculator for children?

This calculator is intended for adults. Do not use its results to assess children or teenagers; seek age-appropriate guidance from a qualified healthcare professional.

Are my measurements uploaded?

No. This calculator performs its calculations in your browser and does not send your measurements to a server.
