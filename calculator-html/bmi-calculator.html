UNDERSTAND YOUR RESULT

What is Body Mass Index?

Body Mass Index (BMI) is a numerical value calculated from a person's weight and height. It is commonly used as an initial screening measure, but it cannot independently determine whether someone is healthy.

ƒ

Metric formula

BMI = Weight (kg) ÷ Height (m)²

Convert height from centimetres to metres before squaring it.

↔

Imperial formula

BMI = 703 × Weight (lb) ÷ Height (in)²

Use total height in inches and weight in pounds.

✓

Example calculation

65 ÷ (1.70 × 1.70) = 22.5

An adult weighing 65 kg at 1.70 m tall has a BMI of approximately 22.5.

COMMON QUESTIONS

Frequently asked questions

Is BMI a diagnosis of my health?

No. BMI is a screening measure. A health professional may consider other information, such as medical history, blood pressure, laboratory findings, fitness, and body composition, when assessing health.

Does BMI work for children and teenagers?

Adult BMI categories should not be used for children and teenagers. Their BMI is interpreted using age- and sex-specific growth charts or percentiles.

Can someone muscular have a high BMI?

Yes. BMI does not distinguish muscle from fat, so it may not reflect body composition accurately in people with substantial muscle mass.

Are my measurements uploaded?

No. This calculator performs the calculation in your browser and does not require your height or weight to be sent to a server.
