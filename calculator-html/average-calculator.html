Definition

What Is an Average?

An average is a single value used to represent a collection of numbers. In everyday mathematics, the word average usually refers to the arithmetic mean.

To calculate the arithmetic mean, add all the numbers together and divide their sum by the number of values in the collection.

Averages are commonly used to understand examination scores, monthly expenses, sales figures, measurements, and many other numerical datasets.

Mathematical Formula

Average Calculator Formula

Use this formula to calculate the arithmetic mean of any valid set of numbers.

Average = Σx / n
Σx The sum of all values
n The total number of values

This formula calculates the arithmetic mean. It is not the same as the median or mode.

Worked Example

How to Calculate an Average

Let's calculate the average of five numbers.

FAQs

Frequently Asked Questions

What does an average calculator do?

It calculates the arithmetic mean by adding all entered numbers and dividing the sum by the number of values.

What is the formula for an average?

Average = Sum of all values divided by the number of values.

Can I calculate the average of decimal numbers?

Yes. Decimal numbers such as 12.5, 18.75, and 20.2 can be entered.

Can I enter negative numbers or zero?

Yes. Zero and negative numbers are valid inputs. The calculator includes them in the sum and count.

What happens if an input field is empty?

The calculator asks you to complete all displayed number fields before calculating. You can remove any unnecessary field.

What is the difference between average and median?

The arithmetic average is the sum divided by the count. The median is the middle value after sorting the numbers. They can produce different results.
