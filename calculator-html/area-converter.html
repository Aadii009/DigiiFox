FORMULAS

Area Conversion Formulas

Understand the basic relationships between area units.

Square metres to square feet

ft² = m² × 10.7639104167

Multiply square metres by approximately 10.76391.

Square feet to square metres

m² = ft² × 0.09290304

Multiply square feet by 0.09290304.

Hectares to square metres

m² = ha × 10,000

One hectare contains exactly 10,000 square metres.

Acres to square metres

m² = acres × 4,046.8564224

Multiply acres by the standard international acre conversion factor.

Square kilometres to hectares

ha = km² × 100

One square kilometre equals 100 hectares.

Square yards to square feet

ft² = yd² × 9

One square yard equals nine square feet.

FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

What is an area converter?

An area converter changes a surface measurement from one unit to another, such as square metres to square feet or acres to hectares.

How many square feet are in one square metre?

One square metre equals approximately 10.76391 square feet.

How many square metres are in one hectare?

One hectare equals exactly 10,000 square metres.

What is the difference between an acre and a hectare?

A hectare is 10,000 square metres. An international acre is approximately 4,046.8564 square metres. One hectare is about 2.47105 acres.

Can I convert land measurements with this tool?

Yes. You can convert between acres, hectares, square metres, and square kilometres. For legal land transactions, verify the local measurement conventions and official records.

Does the converter upload my values?

No. The conversion is calculated directly in your browser. Your entered area value does not need to be uploaded to a server.
