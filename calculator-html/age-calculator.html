UNDERSTANDING AGE

What Is an Age Calculator?

An age calculator is a tool that determines the time elapsed between a person's date of birth and a selected calculation date. It expresses the result in complete years, months, and days.

Unlike a simple division of total days by 365, this calculator accounts for the actual lengths of calendar months and leap years. This makes the calendar age result more accurate.

THE METHOD

Age Calculator Formula

Calendar age is calculated by comparing the birth date with the selected end date.

Age = End Date − Date of Birth

The calculation follows these steps:

  1. Calculate the difference between the two years.
  2. Subtract one year if the birthday has not yet occurred in the end date's year.
  3. Calculate the remaining complete months and days using calendar dates.
  4. Count the total elapsed days to determine total weeks and days.
Important: A calendar month can contain 28, 29, 30, or 31 days. Leap years are also accounted for when calculating calendar age.

COMMON QUESTIONS

Frequently Asked Questions

How is my exact age calculated?

Your age is calculated by comparing your birth date with the selected end date. The result accounts for calendar years, actual month lengths, and leap years.

Can I calculate my age on a past date?

Yes. Choose a date on or after your birth date in the Calculate Age As Of field. The calculator will determine your age on that date.

Does the calculator account for leap years?

Yes. It uses calendar-date calculations, which account for February having 28 or 29 days depending on the year.

How are total weeks calculated?

Total weeks are the number of complete seven-day periods elapsed since your birth. Any remaining days are shown separately.

Is my date of birth stored anywhere?

This calculator performs its calculations locally in your browser. It does not need a server or database to calculate your age.

Why can age in months differ from total days?

Calendar months have different lengths. Therefore, total calendar months and the number of elapsed days measure time differently.
