UNDERSTAND THE BASICS

What Is a Ratio?

A ratio is a mathematical comparison between two or more quantities. It describes how much of one quantity there is in relation to another quantity.

For example, if a basket contains 4 apples and 6 oranges, the ratio of apples to oranges is 4:6. Dividing both terms by 2 simplifies the ratio to 2:3.

Remember: The order of a ratio matters. A ratio of 2:3 is not the same as 3:2.
MATHEMATICAL METHOD

Ratio Calculator Formula

1. Simplifying a Ratio

a : b = (a ÷ GCD) : (b ÷ GCD)

Divide both terms by their greatest common divisor to simplify a whole-number ratio.

2. Finding a Missing Value

a : b = c : x
x = (b × c) ÷ a

This formula solves for x when the proportion is a:b = c:x and a is non-zero.

3. Splitting an Amount

First share = Total × a ÷ (a + b)
Second share = Total × b ÷ (a + b)

Each share is calculated according to its proportion of the total ratio parts.

COMMON QUESTIONS

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the simplest form of a ratio?

It is a ratio whose whole-number terms have no common factor greater than 1. For example, 12:18 simplifies to 2:3.

Can a ratio contain decimal numbers?

Yes. Ratios can compare decimal quantities. This calculator supports decimal inputs for the available calculation modes.

Can I use zero in a ratio?

Zero can be a valid term in some ratios, but both terms cannot be zero when simplifying a ratio. A missing-value calculation also cannot divide by a zero first term.

How do I divide money in a ratio?

Add the ratio terms, divide the total amount by the sum, and multiply by each term to calculate each share.
