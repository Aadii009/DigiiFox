COMMON QUESTIONS

Frequently Asked Questions

Learn more about statistics and the results provided by this calculator.

What statistics does this calculator provide?

It calculates the mean, median, mode, sum, count, minimum, maximum, range, population variance, sample variance, population standard deviation, sample standard deviation, quartiles, and interquartile range.

What is the difference between population and sample variance?

Population variance divides the sum of squared deviations by the total number of population values, N. Sample variance divides by n − 1 to estimate the variance of a larger population from a sample.

What happens if there is no mode?

If every value occurs equally often, the calculator reports no unique mode. If multiple values share the highest frequency, it reports all of those modes.

How are quartiles calculated?

The calculator sorts the data, finds the median, and calculates Q1 and Q3 as the medians of the lower and upper halves. When the dataset contains an odd number of values, the overall median is excluded from both halves.

Can I enter decimal and negative numbers?

Yes. The calculator accepts positive numbers, negative numbers, zero, and decimal values.

Is my data uploaded to a server?

No. This calculator performs its calculations in your browser. It does not send your dataset to a server.

Why are sample statistics unavailable for a single value?

Sample variance divides by n − 1. With only one observation, that denominator is zero, so sample variance and sample standard deviation are undefined.
