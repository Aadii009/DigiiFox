    UNDERSTANDING FRACTIONS

    What is a fraction?

    A fraction represents a part of a whole and is written as one integer over another, such as 3/4. The top number is called the numerator, while the bottom number is called the denominator. The denominator cannot be zero.

    Fractions are used in mathematics, measurements, cooking, financial calculations, and everyday situations. A fraction can often be simplified by dividing its numerator and denominator by their greatest common divisor.

    Fraction formulas

    Learn the basic rules used when calculating fractions.

    Addition

    a/b + c/d = (ad + bc)/bd

    Find a common denominator, add the numerators, and simplify.

    Subtraction

    a/b − c/d = (ad − bc)/bd

    Find a common denominator, subtract the numerators, and simplify.

    Multiplication

    a/b × c/d = ac/bd

    Multiply the numerators and multiply the denominators.

    Division

    a/b ÷ c/d = ad/bc

    Multiply the first fraction by the reciprocal of the second.

    Simplification

    (a ÷ g)/(b ÷ g)

    Divide both numbers by their greatest common divisor, g.

    Decimal conversion

    Decimal = a ÷ b

    Divide the numerator by the denominator to obtain a decimal.

    COMMON QUESTIONS

    Frequently asked questions

    How do I simplify a fraction?

    Divide the numerator and denominator by their greatest common divisor. For example, 6/8 simplifies to 3/4.

    How do I add fractions with different denominators?

    Find a common denominator, convert both fractions to equivalent fractions, add their numerators, and simplify the result.

    What is an improper fraction?

    An improper fraction has a numerator whose absolute value is greater than or equal to its positive denominator. For example, 7/4 is an improper fraction.

    Can a fraction have a denominator of zero?

    No. Division by zero is undefined, so the calculator does not accept a zero denominator.

    How do I convert a fraction into a decimal?

    Divide the numerator by the denominator. For example, 3/4 equals 0.75.
