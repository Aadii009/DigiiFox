The Mathematics

Inflation Calculator Formula

Future Cost =

Current Amount × (1 + Inflation Rate / 100)Years

Inflation generally means that prices rise over time, reducing the purchasing power of money. This calculator applies a constant annual rate with annual compounding to estimate how much a current expense might cost later.

  • Current Amount: Today's cost of the item or service.
  • Inflation Rate: Assumed annual price increase, expressed as a percentage.
  • Years: The number of years in the estimate.
  • Future Cost: Estimated future price after applying inflation.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is an inflation calculator?

An inflation calculator estimates how prices may change over time using an assumed inflation rate. It can help you compare today's costs with potential future costs.

How does inflation affect purchasing power?

When prices rise, the same amount of money buys fewer goods and services. For example, if prices increase by 50%, ₹100 will not buy as much as it did before the increase.

Is the future cost guaranteed?

No. The result is an estimate based on the annual rate you enter. Actual inflation changes over time and varies across products, services, and regions.

Does this calculator use live inflation data?

No. You enter the inflation rate yourself. This calculator does not fetch live inflation data or forecast future economic conditions.

What does future purchasing power mean?

It estimates what a fixed amount of money in the future would be equivalent to in today's purchasing power, using the inflation rate and period you enter.

Does changing the currency convert my money?

No. Changing the currency only changes how results are displayed. It does not convert the entered amount using exchange rates.
