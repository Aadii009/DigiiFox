UNDERSTANDING THE TOOL

What is a scientific calculator?

A scientific calculator is a mathematical tool designed to solve basic arithmetic and more advanced mathematical problems. It includes functions such as trigonometry, logarithms, exponentiation, roots, and factorials.

This online calculator is useful for students, teachers, engineers, and anyone working with mathematical expressions. Choose the appropriate function, enter your values, and calculate your result.

COMMON QUESTIONS

Frequently asked questions

Is this scientific calculator free?

Yes. You can use the calculator directly in your browser without paying or creating an account.

What is the difference between DEG and RAD?

DEG measures angles in degrees, while RAD measures angles in radians. Choose the mode that matches your problem.

Can I calculate sine, cosine, and tangent?

Yes. The calculator supports sin, cos, tan, and their inverse functions. Make sure the angle mode is correct.

Does the calculator support logarithms?

Yes. Use log for base-10 logarithms and ln for natural logarithms. The input must be positive.

Are my calculations uploaded to a server?

The calculator's mathematical operations run in your browser. This page does not send your expressions to a calculation server.

Why does an expression sometimes return an error?

An expression may be invalid, contain unmatched parentheses, divide by zero, or use a function outside its mathematical domain.
