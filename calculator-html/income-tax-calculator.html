UNDERSTANDING TAX

What is an Income Tax Calculator?

An income tax calculator estimates the income tax payable on taxable income after considering applicable deductions, tax slabs, rebates, and cess.

India offers old and new tax regimes with different rates and deduction rules. The most suitable regime depends on your income, eligible deductions, and individual circumstances.

TAX FORMULA

How is income tax calculated?

Step 1: Calculate gross income

Gross income = Salary / pension + other income entered

Step 2: Calculate taxable income

Taxable income = Gross income − standard deduction − eligible deductions

Step 3: Apply the tax slabs

Calculate tax separately for each applicable income band.

Step 4: Apply rebate and cess

Estimated tax = Tax after rebate + applicable cess.

How to use the calculator

IMPORTANT DIFFERENCES

Old vs New Tax Regime

New tax regime

Uses its own slab rates and generally permits fewer exemptions and deductions. Eligible deductions and benefits depend on the applicable law.

Old tax regime

Allows eligible deductions and exemptions, subject to the applicable limits and conditions. Age-based exemption thresholds may apply to resident individuals.

This is a simplified estimator. It does not calculate every deduction, surcharge, special-rate income, or individual tax adjustment.

COMMON QUESTIONS

Frequently Asked Questions

Answers to common questions about income tax.

Which tax year does this calculator use?

The estimate is configured for tax year 2026–27 using the simplified assumptions described on this page. Always verify the rules applicable to your tax year using the official Income Tax Department calculator.

What is the difference between the old and new regimes?

The regimes have different slab rates and rules for deductions and exemptions. Your best option depends on your income and eligible deductions.

Is standard deduction included?

Yes. This estimator applies a configured standard deduction for the selected regime. Verify the amount and eligibility under the rules applicable to you.

Does this calculator include every deduction?

No. The old-regime deductions field accepts a single combined amount. You must ensure that the amount represents eligible deductions and exemptions within the applicable legal limits.

Does the result include cess?

Yes. The estimate includes a 4% health and education cess on the calculated tax after rebate. Other adjustments may affect the final liability.

Is this the final amount I must pay?

No. This is an estimate, not an official tax computation. Your actual liability may differ because of surcharge, special-rate income, additional deductions, TDS, advance tax, marginal relief, and other applicable rules.
