    UNDERSTANDING SQUARE ROOTS

    What Is a Square Root?

    A square root of a number is a value that, when multiplied by itself, produces the original number. The principal square root is the non-negative root.

    Square Root Formula
    √x = y

    This means y × y = x, where x is non-negative and y is the principal square root.

    Example: Square Root of 144

    We want to find a number that gives 144 when multiplied by itself.

    12 × 12 = 144
    √144 = 12

    Important Square Root Rules

    Perfect square √81 = 9 because 9 × 9 = 81.
    Square root of zero √0 = 0.
    Non-perfect square √2 ≈ 1.41421356.
    Squaring and square roots √(x²) = |x| for real numbers.

    Note: The real square root of a negative number is undefined. This calculator accepts only non-negative real numbers.

    COMMON QUESTIONS

    Frequently Asked Questions

    What is a square root?

    A square root is a value that, when multiplied by itself, produces the original number. For example, 5 is the principal square root of 25.

    What is the square root of 144?

    The principal square root of 144 is 12, because 12 × 12 = 144.

    Can I calculate the square root of a decimal?

    Yes. For example, √2.25 = 1.5 because 1.5 × 1.5 = 2.25.

    What is a perfect square?

    A perfect square is a number that can be written as an integer multiplied by itself, such as 16 = 4 × 4.

    Can I calculate the square root of a negative number?

    Not as a real number. The square of any real number is non-negative. Negative inputs require complex numbers, which this calculator does not calculate.

    Is the square root calculator free?

    Yes. You can use this DigiiFox calculator in your browser without installing software.
