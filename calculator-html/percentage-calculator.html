UNDERSTAND THE TOOL

What Is a Percentage?

A percentage represents a value as a fraction of 100. The word percentage means "per hundred" and is represented by the symbol %.

For example, 25% means 25 out of every 100. Percentages are commonly used to calculate discounts, exam scores, interest, price changes, taxes, and business growth.

The DigiiFox Percentage Calculator provides four common percentage calculations in one place, helping you solve everyday numerical problems quickly.

((New − Old) ÷ Old) × 100

Percentage Decrease

((Old − New) ÷ Old) × 100

Example: ((500 − 400) ÷ 500) × 100 = 20%.

Important: Percentage increase and decrease use the original value as the reference. The original value must not be zero. If the new value moves in the opposite direction, the calculator will explain the direction of the change.

Frequently Asked Questions

Answers to common questions about percentages.

How do I calculate a percentage of a number?

Divide the percentage by 100 and multiply the result by the number. For example, 20% of 500 is (20 ÷ 100) × 500 = 100.

How do I find what percentage one number is of another?

Divide the part by the total and multiply by 100. For example, 50 out of 200 is (50 ÷ 200) × 100 = 25%.

How do I calculate percentage increase?

Subtract the original value from the new value, divide by the original value, and multiply by 100.

How do I calculate percentage decrease?

Subtract the new value from the original value, divide by the original value, and multiply by 100. This gives the percentage decrease when the new value is lower than the original.

Can a percentage be greater than 100%?

Yes. A percentage can exceed 100% when the value being compared is greater than the reference value. For example, 150 out of 100 is 150%.

Can I calculate percentages using decimal numbers?

Yes. You can enter decimal values, such as 12.5 or 49.75, into the calculator.

Is this calculator free and private?

Yes. The calculator is free to use and performs its calculations in your browser. It does not send the entered numbers to a calculation server.
